# Offline city labels

Derived from GeoNames cities15000, downloaded 2026-11-02:
https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip

GeoNames data is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Attribution: GeoNames (https://www.geonames.org/).

Only city name, coordinates, and country code are retained. This is an approximate nearest indexed city, not city boundary membership or an address. Places more than 100 km from an indexed city show city unavailable. The static dataset is loaded from our own frontend; device coordinates remain in browser memory and never appear in lookup requests. Search scope and center distance calculations remain unchanged.
