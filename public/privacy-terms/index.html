Terms & Privacy

How MySelfPay handles information and the conditions for using the service.

Updated October 30, 2026

Who operates this service

MySelfPay is operated by One Smarter, Inc. For privacy questions, contact care@onesmarter.com.

Guest searches and chat

Search details and chat messages are sent to our server to process your request. In the current guest flow, conversation text and searched locations are not saved as conversation history. Chat text stays in the open page and disappears on refresh.

Optional accounts

You can continue using Sign in as guest or create an account with your chosen username, name, password and mobile number. Your five-digit ZIP code is optional. We store a salted password hash, not your password. Account details remain until you request deletion or another applicable retention requirement applies. Account sessions last up to eight hours. Registration requires mobile verification through Twilio Verify. Once your mobile is verified and you are logged in, you can request a time without verifying it again. We do not ask patients for an email address. Search and chat remain private in this interface and are not automatically saved. Automated password recovery is not available yet; contact care@onesmarter.com for help without sending your password.

Browser navigation

To restore your page after refresh or Back/Forward, this browser tab keeps search filters, selected listings and directory view settings in its history state for up to eight hours. These settings are tied to the signed-in session’s member and are not placed in the page URL. Passwords, chat messages, appointment forms and verification codes are not included. Signing out prevents restoring those settings while signed out. Search results are fetched again from the current directory.

Sessions and usage limits

A necessary session cookie keeps your guest session active for up to eight hours. We store guest session records and usage counters to operate the service and enforce limits. The application uses hashed request identifiers for rate limiting. Hosting providers may process technical request information, including IP addresses and access logs.

Appointment request preview

When you submit an appointment request, we store your first name, verified account mobile number, clinic, consent and up to three preferred time windows. Mobile verification confirms access to the number, not a person's identity. New requests do not collect a patient email or create a secure conversation. The center receives a notification through our SMTP provider when delivery is enabled and must verify its own email to review the request. A request does not reserve an appointment; the center confirms the appointment directly with you. Historical account and request records are retained according to applicable retention requirements.

Booking details are encrypted in the database. The private patient link shows request status; keep it private. Clinic links expire after 72 hours, require a one-time code and grant a 15-minute session. The local development server exposes a clearly labeled clinic preview and test codes for testing only; these are not available on hosted deployments.

Requests close when marked scheduled or unable to accommodate, or expire after 72 hours if still open. Details are deleted 30 days after closing by the retention task. The local server runs this task at startup and hourly while running. Backup copies follow the operator’s separate backup retention policy.

Browser storage

A service worker may cache static application resources for offline use. Clearing this site's browser data removes locally stored resources and cookies; it does not itself delete server records.

Maps and outside links

Facility maps load from OpenStreetMap only after you select Load map. That service receives your IP address and the selected facility map coordinates. Your search location is not included in the map request. Call, website and directions links open external services whose own privacy practices apply.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and message to respond. Do not include patient records or sensitive medical details. Contact us about access, correction or deletion requests; we may need to verify the request and consider applicable retention obligations.

Use of this review service

Do not enter sensitive personal or medical information into the current review experience. No real document upload, OCR or external speech service is connected.

About the service

MySelfPay, operated by One Smarter, Inc., provides directory information and tools for exploring self-pay imaging options. It does not provide medical diagnosis, recommend an exam or replace advice from your clinician.

Confirm directly with the facility

Published prices are references and may change. Confirm the exact exam, contrast, included fees, eligibility, order requirements and all-in price before arranging care. Listings do not guarantee availability, payment acceptance or savings over insurance.

Contact and bookings

Calls, facility websites and directions use external services. MySelfPay does not book appointments, collect payment or send patient requests to facilities in the current experience. The price-request tool provides questions for your own conversation with a facility.

Responsible use

Use the service lawfully. Do not submit sensitive patient information, misuse another person's information, disrupt the service or attempt to bypass access controls and usage limits.

Corrections and questions

Report inaccurate listings or ask about these terms at care@onesmarter.com. Facility participation and any paid services require separately agreed terms.

Privacy and data handling

What we process and why

We process the exam, contrast preference, city or ZIP code and optional budget you submit to return relevant directory results. Guest chat messages are processed to respond to your request. Avoid names, dates of birth, insurance numbers, medical records and other sensitive patient details.

Session expiry and retention

Session expiry limits access; it does not mean every server record is immediately erased. Session records, usage counters, technical logs and correspondence may have different retention periods. Contact us to ask about retained information or request deletion. Clearing browser data removes local cookies and cached files only.

Service providers and external destinations

Hosting and database services support operation of MySelfPay and may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service. Facility websites, telephone services and map providers operate independently. Review their policies before submitting information to them.

Your choices

You can browse these information pages without a guest session, choose not to load a map, and stop using the service at any time. Blocking necessary cookies may prevent guest features from working. For privacy requests, contact care@onesmarter.com without including sensitive records in your initial email.

Using MySelfPay

Directory scope and accuracy

Entries can represent a facility, multiple locations or a program. A state listed in program coverage does not establish a physical facility in that state. Distances may use city or ZIP reference points and are not driving distances. Confirm the address, service and access requirements with the facility.

Prices and comparisons

Compare the same body part, exam protocol and contrast requirements. Starting prices, ranges, screening packages and diagnostic exams may not be interchangeable. Ask whether the quoted total includes the radiologist, facility, contrast, supplies and any additional required services. Self-pay is not necessarily less expensive than using coverage.

No booking or payment agreement

Viewing a listing or using the call script does not reserve an appointment or create an agreement with a facility. Appointment, payment, cancellation, refund and insurance terms come from the facility. Any participation agreement with One Smarter is handled separately.

Availability and updates

Features, listings and published information may change. We may restrict misuse or suspend features for maintenance and security. These terms do not promise uninterrupted access or a particular medical or financial outcome.

Questions and corrections

Send listing corrections, accessibility concerns and questions about this page to care@onesmarter.com. Include the listing name and public source link where possible. Do not include patient information. The date on this page indicates its latest update.
